Basalt Notes privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Your notes

Basalt Notes stores your notes on your device. The developer runs no servers and never receives your notes or any other information from the app.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Basalt Notes asks for Google's drive.file permission. It can then see and change only the files it creates or that you open with it, and nothing else in your Drive. It uses this access only to save your notes to your own Drive and keep them in sync between your devices. It also reads the email address of the connected account so it can show which account is in use.

Your sign-in is stored encrypted on your device and is sent only to Google. You can remove Basalt Notes' access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Basalt Notes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Basalt Notes does not do

It shows no ads, collects no analytics, does not track you, and does not sell or share your information with anyone.

Deleting your information

Delete notes in the app, or uninstall it, to remove them from your device. Notes saved to your Google Drive stay there until you delete them from Drive.

Changes and questions

Changes to this policy are posted on this page. For questions, open an issue at github.com/muncheslunches/basalt-notes/issues.
